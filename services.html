On-Demand Hourly Service

Our standard hourly option provides flexible, on-demand access to high-level technical skills, pedagogical expertise, and administrative strategy without a long-term tier commitment. This structure covers short-term tactical projects including data privacy audits, software spend analysis, user account lifecycle systems, and leadership policy consultations.

Scope Exclusion: Hourly frameworks strictly exclude the development and presentation of formal, large-group professional development courses or faculty workshops, which require specialized backend curriculum engineering.

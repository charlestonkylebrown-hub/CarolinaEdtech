Executive Service Options

Flexible fractional leadership models allowing institutions to scale executive tech oversight based on institutional needs.

★ Anchor Level ★

Tier 1: Edtech Director-in-Residence

A strategic, fractional leadership model for administrative-level innovation and specialized technology coaching.

  • • Time Commitment: 2 days per week (8 days/mo) on-site; 20 hours off-site.
  • • Ethical Governance: Creation of human-centered tech & AI policies.
  • • Instructional Integration: Monthly dedicated 1-on-1 or small group coaching.
  • • Professional Development: Includes 2 individual sessions or one 3-hour course per year, plus a 25% discount on additional courses.
  • • Lifecycle Management: Full management of user accounts, licenses, and audits.

Tier 2: Edtech Partner

Premium Level

Fractional leadership and expert coaching providing executive-level guidance without full-time costs.

  • • Time Commitment: 1 day per week (4 days/mo) on-site; 20 hours off-site.
  • • Administrative Partnership: Alignment of tech with institutional goals.
  • • Instructional Integration: Monthly 1-on-1 or small group instructional coaching.
  • • PD Savings: 20% discount on all full professional development training.
  • • Lifecycle Management: User account and license renewal management.

Tier 3: Edtech Specialist

Operational Stability

Designed for schools needing technical consistency and back-end health to ensure essential digital infrastructure remains functional and secure.

  • • Time Commitment: 3 days per month on-site; up to 10 hours off-site.
  • • Managed Upkeep: Hardware/software troubleshooting, account management.
  • • Individualized Sessions: Monthly 1-on-1 coaching for enhanced student engagement.
  • • PD Savings: 10% discount on full professional development courses.
  • • Culture Building: Monthly "Chat-n-Share" segments during faculty meetings.
